WHO WE ARE

Small steps.
Meaningful change.

Root & Rise is an organic farming concept built around a simple idea: healthy food and healthy ecosystems belong together.

We believe a farm should be a living system. Soil, water, plants, insects, animals and people all play a part. Our approach values natural cycles, thoughtful planning and respect for the land.

Whether you are a home cook, a local shop or someone curious about sustainable agriculture, we invite you to be part of the journey.
